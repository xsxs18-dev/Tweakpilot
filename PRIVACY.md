# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

TweakPilot does not collect, store or send any personal data.

It has no network access, no analytics and no tracking. Everything it does happens only on your device:

- listing the tweaks installed on your device
- showing RAM, CPU and battery usage
- turning tweaks on or off
- respringing or restarting injection

The only thing TweakPilot saves is the size of the panel and the position of the bubble, so they stay the same after a respring. This stays on your device.

## Support

If you contact me for support, for example on GitHub or by email, I only use your message to help you. I don't share it with anyone.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here.
